## 3. Plan for this article

### P1: do first

#### 3.1 Retarget the title and H1 to forex

Current title: `High-Frequency Trading in 2025: How to Balance Speed and Risk?`

Problems: stale year, no "forex", and it targets generic HFT while the forex modifiers sit at 62 to 76.

Recommended title: `High-Frequency Trading in Forex: What It Is and How It Works (2026)`

Recommended H1: `What Is High-Frequency Trading in Forex?`

Recommended subhead: `How HFT works, why retail traders cannot compete, and what to do instead.`

Recommended meta: `High-frequency trading in forex explained: latency, strategies, why retail traders cannot compete, and the broker conditions that matter for an active style.`

Keep the exact phrase `high frequency trading` in the title so the page does not lose its 9.4 ranking for the broad term.

#### 3.2 Make "What is high-frequency trading in forex?" the definition H2

Current H2 is "What is High Frequency Trading?" and the forex phrase appears once in the intro.

New H2: `What is high-frequency trading in forex?`

Use these exact phrases in the first 100 words:

- `high-frequency trading in forex`
- `high frequency trading forex`
- `forex high frequency trading`
- `FX high frequency trading`

Explain the split: HFT is institutional, latency-sensitive trading, not a retail strategy. Keep the current definition of algorithms, milliseconds and tiny price moves.

#### 3.3 Add "How fast is high-frequency trading?"

Covers:
- `how fast is high frequency trading` (4 impressions)
- `high speed trading` (3)

Suggested table:

| Trader type | Typical reaction or execution |
|---|---|
| Human click | 200 to 300 ms |
| Retail platform | 50 to 100 ms |
| Retail VPS | 10 to 50 ms |
| HFT colocated system | microseconds |

Label these as broad ranges, not guarantees. Add one sentence: speed alone does not create an edge; costs and infrastructure do.

#### 3.4 Rename the strategies section to match the query

Current H2: "Strategies That Fuel HFT:".

New H2: `High-frequency forex trading strategy: 5 common approaches`

Use the exact phrases:

- `high frequency forex trading strategy`
- `high frequency trading strategies`
- `high frequency strategies`

The five strategies already in the article are enough: market making, arbitrage, statistical arbitrage, trend, news-based. Add a one-line "who uses it" for each (institution, prop desk, market maker) to support the institutional query.

#### 3.5 Strengthen the retail reality section

Current angle is correct: retail traders cannot compete. Make it a proper H2:

`Why retail traders cannot compete with high-frequency trading`

Cover:

- latency and colocation
- commission and spread costs at high trade counts
- broker rules that prohibit HFT or latency arbitrage
- capital required for infrastructure
- why "high frequency scalping" is not HFT

This also sets up the honest answer to `how to high frequency trade`.

#### 3.6 Add "How to trade a high-frequency style as a retail trader"

Covers:
- `how to high frequency trade` (2 impressions, position 89.5)

Answer: you do not run HFT. You can run an active scalping style with a low-cost broker, tight spreads, a fixed risk per trade and a daily loss cap. Add a short list of what actually matters: spreads, commissions, execution, VPS, and risk limits. Link to the lot size calculator and Guardian.

Explain: HFT firms do not sell signal subscriptions; they trade their own capital and infrastructure. A "high frequency signal" sold to retail is usually a scalping alert, not HFT. This protects the brand from being confused with signal sellers.

The individuals/stocks answer covers the long query at position 7. Keep it to 50 to 70 words.

Criteria: raw spreads, commission per lot, execution speed, order type support, API access, HFT/latency policy, account restrictions. Do not name or rank brokers here. Link to the separate broker guide.
