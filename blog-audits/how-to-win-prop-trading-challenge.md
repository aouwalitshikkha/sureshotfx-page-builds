## 3. Plan for this article

### P1: do first

#### 3.1 Retarget the title and H1 from "win" to "pass"

- Current title: `How to Win Prop Trading Challenge? - SureShotFX`
- Recommended title: `How to Pass a Prop Trading Challenge: 7-Step Plan (2026)`
- Recommended H1: `How to Pass a Prop Trading Challenge in 7 Steps`
- Recommended meta: `A practical 7-step plan to pass a prop trading challenge: the rules that fail accounts, how to size risk, build one strategy and protect drawdown with Guardian.`

Why: there is no `how to win` query in the GSC set. The `how to pass` cluster has six variants, including one at position 7. The current title also has a grammar error, missing "a".

Keep "win" in the intro, for example: "Winning a prop challenge is not about one big trade. It is about not losing the account before the target."

#### 3.2 Add "What is a prop trading challenge?"

Format: 60 to 90 word definition, followed by a rules table.

Suggested table:

| Rule | What it means | Typical example |
|---|---|---|
| Profit target | The gain required to pass | 8% to 10% |
| Max daily loss | The most you can lose in one day | 5% |
| Max overall loss | The most you can lose in total | 10% |
| Minimum days | Days you must trade before a payout | 3 to 10 |
| Time limit | How long the challenge lasts | 30 days |
| Consistency rule | Limits on one unusually large day | 30% to 40% of profit |

Exact phrases to include:

- `what is a prop trading challenge`
- `what is prop challenge`
- `prop challenge`
- `prop challenges`

This covers four queries at once. Do not invent firm-specific rules; label the figures as examples.

#### 3.3 Add "How to pass a prop trading challenge in 7 steps"

Numbered H2, one short paragraph per step:

1. Pick the firm and read the rulebook.
2. Set risk per trade from the daily loss limit.
3. Set a daily loss budget and stop at it.
4. Trade one strategy, not five.
5. Choose one session and one or two pairs.
6. Journal every trade, including the rule you followed.
7. Review at the end of each day and cut what fails.

Use these exact phrases in the intro and FAQ:

- `how to pass a prop trading challenge`
- `how to pass prop trading challenge`
- `how to pass prop firm challenge`
- `how to successfully pass a prop firm trading challenge`

Add a short forex example: `forex prop firm challenge`, `prop firm challenge forex`, `prop firm forex challenge`. One example is enough; do not repeat the phrase in every paragraph.

#### 3.4 Use "prop firm trading challenge" exactly

It has 31 impressions and no exact match. Put it in the H1 or an H2, not both:

`How to Pass a Prop Firm Trading Challenge in 7 Steps`

If the H1 uses "prop trading challenge", add "prop firm trading challenge" to the meta or the first sentence.

Covers:

Answer honestly: there are no secrets. The "secret" is staying inside the daily and overall loss limits and taking one repeatable setup. This satisfies the query without clickbait.

- `can i journal my way to passing a prop challenge 2026` (1 impression, position 10)

Risk table columns: account size, daily loss limit, risk per trade, max trades per day. Example row: $10,000 account, 5% daily limit = $500, 1% risk per trade = $100, maximum 5 losses.

Journal checklist: date, pair, session, setup, risk, result, rule followed, screenshot.

If the client later creates a dedicated journaling article, this section can be shortened and linked instead.
