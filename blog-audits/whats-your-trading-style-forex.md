## 3. Plan for this article

### P1: do first

#### 3.1 Add a "What is a trading style?" definition

Current first H2 is "FOREX Trading Strategies", which is the wrong label and does not match the query.

New H2: `What is a forex trading style?`

Use these exact phrases in the first 80 words:

- `forex trading style`
- `forex style`
- `trading style`
- `trading styles in forex`

Definition: a trading style is the set of rules that decides how long you hold a trade, how often you trade and which timeframe you use. It is not the same as a strategy; the style is the time horizon and rhythm, the strategy is the setup.

#### 3.2 Rename the styles section and add a comparison table

Current H2: `FOREX Trading Strategies`

New H2: `The four main forex trading styles`

Keep `forex trading styles` exact in the intro sentence.

Add a table immediately after the intro:

| Style | Holding period | Screen time | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scalping | Seconds to minutes | Very high | Fast, mechanical traders |
| Day trading | Minutes to hours, closed by the end of the day | High | Traders with a full trading session free |
| Swing trading | Several days | Low to medium | People with a day job |
| Position trading | Weeks to years | Low | Patient, fundamentals-led traders |

The article already has pros and cons for each style. The table is the missing extractable summary.

#### 3.3 Add "What helps determine your ideal trading style?"

Covers:
- `what helps determine your ideal trading style` (1 impression, position 60)

Turn the existing self-assessment content into a direct H2 with six factors:

1. Time available each day
2. Risk tolerance
3. Personality and patience
4. Account size
5. Experience level
6. Lifestyle and screen access

Then keep the existing "Self-Assessment, Research, Practice, Evaluate, Education" list as a short action plan under it.

#### 3.4 Fix the meta description and title

Current meta says "day trader, swing trader or swinger". Replace with "position trader".

Recommended title: `Forex Trading Styles: Day, Scalping, Swing & Position`

Why: it contains `forex trading styles`, and `forex trading style` is a substring of it, so the singular head term is preserved. It also names the four styles, which is what the page delivers.

Recommended H1: `Forex Trading Styles: Find the One That Fits You`

Recommended meta: `Compare the four main forex trading styles, see what determines your ideal style, and learn how time, risk tolerance and personality change the answer.`
