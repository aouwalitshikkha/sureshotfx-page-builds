## 3. Plan for this article

### P1: do first

#### 3.1 Retarget the title and H1 to "forex trading plan"

Current title: `How to Create a Good Trading Plan? Beginner's Guide`
Current H1: `How to Create a Good Trading Plan?`

The word "forex" never appears next to "trading plan", yet `forex trading plan` is the largest query (30 impressions).

Recommended title: `How to Create a Forex Trading Plan: Beginner's Guide (2026)`

Recommended H1: `How to Create a Forex Trading Plan`

Recommended meta: `A step-by-step guide to building a forex trading plan: chart context, setups, stop loss, risk, day-trading rules and a review routine.`

Keep `how to create a trading plan` in the body so the existing exact match is not lost.

#### 3.2 Make the main H2 cover create, make and build

Current H2: `How to create a trading plan?`

Recommended H2: `How to make a trading plan (create, build, write)`

Use the exact phrases in the intro and FAQ:

- `how to make a trading plan` (15 impressions)
- `how to build a trading plan` (2, position 5.0)
- `how to write a trading plan` (3)
- `build trading plan` (2)
- `how to create trading plan` and `how to make trading plan` (missing "a"; fix naturally)

One H2 plus a FAQ answer can cover all of them without repeating the phrase in every paragraph.

#### 3.3 Add "What is a forex trading plan?" as the definition H2

Current H2: `What is a trading plan?`

Recommended H2: `What is a forex trading plan?`

Define it in 60 to 80 words: the written rules for markets, timeframe, setup, entry, stop, target, risk, session and review. Include `forex trading plan` and `trading plan for beginners` in the answer.

#### 3.4 Fix the "5 main factors" structure

The article says there are five factors but only numbers three. The remaining topics (analytical approach, setups, markets, holding period, risk tolerance, adversity, routine) are unheaded paragraphs.

Turn them into H3s:

- `1. Choose your analytical approach`
- `2. Select your trade setups`
- `3. Limit the markets you follow`
- `4. Decide your holding period`
- `5. Set your risk tolerance`
- `6. Plan for drawdown and success`
- `7. Build a review routine`

Then either change "5 main factors" to "7 factors" or group them under five headings. The mismatch is a credibility problem and makes the page harder to extract.

#### 3.5 Add a day-trading plan section

Covers:
- `how to create day trading plan` (4)
- `create day trading plan` (3)

Add `Day trading plan example` as an H2 or H3 with a small table:

| Element | Example |
|---|---|
| Session | London open |
| Markets | EURUSD, GBPUSD |
| Setup | Pullback to structure |
| Max trades per day | 2 |
| Risk per trade | 1% |
| Daily stop | 2 losses or -2% |
| News rule | No entries 15 minutes around high-impact news |

Label it as an example, not a recommendation.

### P2: next

#### 3.6 Add a one-page trading plan template

The article has no template or checklist. Add a compact checklist:

- markets and sessions
- timeframe and trend filter
- setup and entry trigger
- stop loss and take profit
- risk per trade and daily stop
- news rules
- review routine

This supports `trading plan guide` (4 impressions) and gives the page a reason to be bookmarked. A downloadable PDF is optional; the on-page checklist is enough.

#### 3.7 Add 8 to 10 FAQs and `FAQPage` schema

Suggested questions:

1. What is a forex trading plan?
2. How do I make a trading plan?
3. How long should a trading plan be?
4. Do I need a separate day trading plan?
5. What risk per trade should a beginner use?
6. How often should I review the plan?
7. What is the difference between a trading plan and a strategy?
8. Can a trading plan guarantee profit?
9. How do I stop breaking my own rules?

The last two keep the page honest: a plan improves consistency, it does not guarantee profit.

#### 3.8 Add the "beginner" phrase

Covers:
- `trading plan for beginners` (3, position 77)

Use it in a short H2 or in the definition: "A trading plan for beginners should start with one market, one setup and a fixed risk per trade."

#### 3.9 Use "profitable forex trading plan" carefully

Covers:
- `profitable forex trading plan` (2)

Do not promise profit. Use it in a question: "What does a profitable forex trading plan look like?" The answer should say profitability comes from consistency and risk control, not from the plan alone.
