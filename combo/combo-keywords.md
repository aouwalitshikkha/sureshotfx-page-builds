## 5. Content rules used

- **One intent per block.** Pricing sells the bundle, markets explain the three desks, value proves the maths, FAQ removes objections.
- **The bundle is the product, not a discount banner.** "Combo signals", "VIP combo" and "combo bundle" appear in the H1, hero and body copy — not only in the plan card.
- **Sibling terms are routed, not absorbed.** `forex vip signals`, `gold vip signals` and `vip signal forex` link to the dedicated pages so the combo does not compete with them.
- **Price is stated once, consistently.** Cards say $129.99 / $189.99 / $299.99; the FAQ repeats those figures; the old $99/$349 FAQ copy is not carried over.
- **Claims stay qualified.** 4.7 Myfxbook, 40–55 signals/week and the 2019 start date are sourced from the live page; no accuracy percentage or pip/point total is invented for the bundle.
- **Brand variants live in low-friction UI.** Ten spellings are absorbed by the brand FAQ and the popular-searches chips without polluting sales copy.
