- **Handles only, no personal names.** No photos, no real names, no allegations beyond impersonation.

- **A correction route.** Anyone can contact support to dispute a listing.
- **No index, no SEO push.** `noindex, follow`; no sitemap entry; no internal "SEO" links beyond the footer.
- **Dates.** The page says when it was updated. The handle list must be reviewed before each update.
- **No non-English lines, no Popular searches, no em dashes.**
