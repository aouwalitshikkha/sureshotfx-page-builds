Red flags

What SureShotFX never asks for

Every impersonation report we receive follows the same pattern: a request for money or credentials outside the official checkout. If someone asks for any of the items below, stop and report the account.

The only legitimate payment is the service charge shown on this website. There is no "release fee", "verification fee" or "extra service charge" on top of it.

Payment to a personal account

Bank transfer, mobile money, UPI or a wallet address sent in a DM.

Crypto "to unlock" your access

No crypto transfer releases signals, a licence or a withdrawal.

Card details in Telegram

Card data belongs in the website checkout, never in a chat.

An extra service charge

The price on the site is the full price. There is no release fee.

Your MT4/MT5 password in a chat

The copier does not need your broker password to place trades in your account.

A guarantee of profit

No honest provider can promise a return. Treat it as a sales red flag.

Take action

How do you report a fake SureShotFX account?

Stop the conversation first. Reporting takes four steps, and no step requires you to send money.

01

Stop and do not pay

Do not send money, card details or account credentials. Do not click links the account sends.

02

Capture the evidence

Screenshot the profile, the exact handle, the messages and any payment request or wallet address.

03

Report inside Telegram

Use the app's report option on the profile or message. Telegram handles the platform side.

04

Send it to support

Send the screenshots and handle to @ssf_adminbot. We add confirmed reports to this page.

If money was already sent: contact your bank or payment provider immediately, report the transaction and file a report with your local police. SureShotFX cannot recover funds sent to a third party, but a fast bank report is the best chance of a chargeback.

+

No. Payment is made through the checkout on this website for the service charge shown on the plan. We do not ask for transfers to personal accounts, wallets, UPI or mobile money in a Telegram chat.
