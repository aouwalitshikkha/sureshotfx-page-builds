## 1. The decision

Old target: the product name only.
New target: **smart money concepts book** and **SMC trading PDF**, with the product name as the brand.

Why: the brand term already ranks 5.7, but `smc trading pdf` sits at 99 and the page has no category visibility. The content is a complete SMC framework, so the category is the honest target.

## 5. Content rules

- **Brand plus category in the H1.** "Behind the Candle" stays first; "The Smart Money Concepts Book" follows on a new line.
- **Definitions are explicit.** Six SMC terms get a heading and a one-sentence definition, so AI answers can lift them.
- **The summary is a feature.** The five-chapter overview is labelled "Book summary" and mentions summarising the book, matching the AI-style query.
- **Claims stay tied to the source.** Five chapters, 10+ examples, the 1:2 to 1:4 target and the 50% price cut come from the live page. No page count is invented.
- **LLM-friendly structure.** TL;DR, question headings, summary block, definitions, risk table, 10-question FAQ and matching schema.
- **No em dashes or AI filler.**
