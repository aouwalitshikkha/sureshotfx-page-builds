## 5. Content rules used

- **One intent per block.** Why = the trade lifecycle, features = the eight tools, how = the path from checkout to first copy, compatibility = "will it run on my setup".
- **The product is the page, not the brand.** "Trade copier", "MT4", "MT5" and "forex signals" appear in the H1, meta and first paragraph. The brand term is carried by the header, footer and FAQ instead.
- **Price is stated once, consistently.** $49.99 lifetime against a $74.99 strike, coupon `FRIDAYCOPY`, 33% saving — the same numbers in the card, the sticky CTA, the banner and the JSON-LD.
- **The news filter is not faked.** The copier has no economic-calendar filter; the FAQ says so and links to `/algo`, which does.
- **Claims stay qualified.** Execution speed, "under two minutes", "3 accounts" and "free updates" come from the live page. No accuracy percentage, pip total or star rating is invented.
- **Brand variants live in low-friction UI.** Fourteen spellings are absorbed by FAQ 15 and the chips without polluting sales copy.
