**What the old page was doing:** it ranked almost entirely on the brand (`sureshotfx`, 1,131 impressions at 1.1) and picked up product impressions at position 78–96 — page two or worse — because the words "trade copier" appear, but never the query strings people type. It also carried two FAQ answers about other products (SSF Guardian and SureShotFX Algo), which is where the `trade copier news filter` and Algo intent leaked in without ever being answered.

**What the new page does differently:**

1. Puts the product in the H1 ("Copy Every VIP Signal / Straight To MT4 & MT5") and the exact category phrases in body copy — `forex signal trade copier`, `forex signal copier service`, `tradecopier`.
2. Answers `trade copier news filter` explicitly and routes that reader to `/algo` instead of leaving them on a page that cannot help.
3. Answers the four questions the live page never addressed: what the copier is, whether it is included in VIP, which brokers/account types, and whether this is the official product.
4. Absorbs 11 brand spellings in the brand FAQ and the popular-searches block, so the brand defence does not depend on the H1.

**What still needs work**

1. `sureshotfx` is 85% of impressions. The product queries are tiny today (13 + 10 impressions). Track `forex signal trade copier` and `trade copier news filter` for 90 days — the FAQ and the new copy are aimed at moving those from position ~79 into the top 30.
2. `site:sureshotfx.com` (20 impressions) means Google crawls this URL as a brand page. Internal links from `/forex-vip-signals`, `/gold-vip-signals`, `/combo` and the blog will do more than further on-page edits.
3. Two queries (`sureshot academy`, `the sure fx group`) may be SureShotFX properties. If the client confirms, they are one sentence away from being covered.
4. `sure shot stock calls` and `sure shot equity calls` rank well (3.5 and 9.0) and deserve a home — just not on the copier page.
