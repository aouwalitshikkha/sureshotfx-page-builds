The unit

What Is a Pip — and What Is It Worth?

A pip is the smallest standard move in a forex quote. On most pairs it is the fourth decimal place: EURUSD moving from 1.0850 to 1.0851 is one pip. On pairs quoted in yen it is the second decimal: USDJPY from 150.00 to 150.01.

The value of that pip depends on the contract size and the quote currency. One standard lot of EURUSD pays $10 per pip; a micro lot pays $0.10. That is why a one-pip move can be $10 on one position and ten cents on another — and why traders search for a one pip forex app or a one pip trading app to check before they click.

Gold and indices do not use the same convention. The SureShotFX app counts one gold pip as 0.10, so a 0.10 lot is about $1 per pip. Index CFDs are counted in points, and the value depends on the contract size you select.

Pip value formula

Pip value = pip size × contract size × quote-to-account rate

For EURUSD: 0.0001 × 100,000 = $10 per standard lot. The app does the conversion for JPY pairs and crosses.
