## 6. Content rules used

- **Store facts come from the store.** Features, instruments and the buy/sell example are taken from the Google Play listing and the shipping screenshots — not invented.
- **Nothing unverifiable is claimed.** No "ad-free", no "works offline", no iOS availability, no download or rating number without a source.
- **The iOS gap is stated, not hidden.** The dead App Store badge was removed instead of being left as a broken download button.
- **Pip maths stays instrument-specific.** Forex uses 0.0001/0.01, gold uses 0.10 in the app's convention, and indices are labelled broker-dependent.
- **The app is a calculator, not a broker.** Every relevant section says it does not place trades or ask for MT4/MT5 credentials.
