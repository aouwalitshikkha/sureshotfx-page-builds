## 5. Content rules

- **Category first, product second.** The H1 starts with the product name, then states the category in the same heading.
- **No fake specifics.** 81 pages and six chapter names come from the cover's contents page. No author name is invented; the book is attributed to the SureShotFX desk, as on the live page.
- **One H1.** The live page had four; the rebuild has one.
- **The blog stays the list hub.** The product page links to Behind the Candle and VIP signals, not to a "best books" list.
- **LLM-friendly structure.** TL;DR in the first screen, question headings, chapter table, key takeaways, 10-question FAQ with matching schema.
- **No em dashes or AI filler.**
