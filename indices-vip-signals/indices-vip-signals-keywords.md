## 6. Content rules used

- **One intent per block.** Hero sells the product, the index section explains coverage, the comparison table removes the free-channel objection.
- **Exact variants in body copy, not just headings.** `vip signals`, `index signals`, `index vip`, `vip access` and `indices signals telegram` appear in sentences where they read naturally.
- **Claims are qualified.** 85–95% accuracy and 2,000–3,000 points/month always sit next to a risk line. No guaranteed-profit language.
- **Brand variants live in low-friction UI.** The popular-searches chips absorb eight brand spellings and two price variants without polluting sales copy.
- **Sibling intent is routed, not cannibalised.** Forex and gold queries link out; the indices page keeps a single market focus.
