How an indices VIP signal is built

4 checks
  1. 01Macro filter: session bias, VIX, yields, DXY and the day's economic calendar.
  2. 02Session timing: Asia range, London open, New York cash open or the closing hour.
  3. 03Level quality: prior day high/low, opening range, VWAP, volume shelf, round number.
  4. 04Risk check: stop behind invalidation, minimum 1:1.5 to TP1, no trades into red-folder news.

Index results are measured in points (1.0 index point), not forex pips. Every level is quoted exactly as it appears on MT4/MT5 index CFDs.

What makes index trading different from forex?

+

Stock indices are driven by corporate earnings, US economic data (NFP, GDP, CPI), Federal Reserve policy and broad risk sentiment. They trade primarily inside US equity hours (9:30am–4:00pm ET), which concentrates volatility into a few windows. Index signals therefore account for session timing, the earnings calendar and equity-specific levels that standard forex analysis misses.
