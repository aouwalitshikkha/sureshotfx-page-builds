## 5. Content rules used

- **One intent per block.** Each section answers one search family; no section tries to rank for three.
- **Exact variants in body copy, not just headings.** Word-order variants (`gold signals vip`, `vip gold signals`) appear in sentences where they read naturally.
- **Claims are qualified.** ~90% accuracy and 2,000–3,000 pips sit next to a risk line — the page targets commercial queries without making guaranteed-profit claims.
- **Brand variants live in low-friction UI.** The popular-searches chips handle 10 brand variants without polluting the sales copy.
- **Internal links carry the cluster.** Forex, Indices and Combo are linked from XAUUSD, pricing, FAQ and footer, so the gold page supports the other service pages instead of competing with them.
