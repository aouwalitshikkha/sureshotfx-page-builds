**Why the old page plateaued:** the live page repeats "Gold VIP Signals" in the hero, pricing and FAQ, but has no section for `gold signals vip`, `gold vip pro`, `gold vip signals telegram`, `free gold signals telegram`, `best xauusd signal provider` or `gold vip signals logo` — and it never spells the brand the way 57 impressions of traffic does (`sureshot fx`). Those clusters sit at positions 4–10, where small on-page additions move rankings.

**What still needs work to close the last gaps**

1. Add "200 USD" and "all wallet payments" as literal strings near the pricing footnote if those queries keep appearing.
2. Re-check Persian/Arabic copy with a native speaker (the Arabic support line is written, not translated by the dev).
3. Keep the brand variants in the popular-searches block in sync with the monthly GSC export.
