## 3. What changed in the design

1. **Keyword intent split into sections** — each query family gets its own block instead of being stacked in the hero: pricing (price intent), pairs (product intent), sessions (timing intent), compare table (free-vs-VIP intent), FAQ (informational intent).
2. **Black Friday preserved and expanded** — top announcement bar with live countdown, a dedicated sale hero inside `#pricing`, coupon copy buttons (`BFFOREX79`, `BFFOREX129`), and a sticky mobile CTA that always carries the 72% offer.
3. **CTA hierarchy** — "Join Forex VIP Signals" (hero) → plan cards → combo upsell → sticky bar. One primary action per screen.
4. **Trust stack** — 12,400 traders / 4.7 Myfxbook / 2–8 signals / 24/7 support strip under the hero, performance panel with 30-day stats, 6 reviews, "As featured on" marquee.
5. **Technical SEO** — single H1, descriptive H2/H3 per topic, canonical, OG/Twitter tags, `Product` + `FAQPage` + `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD, internal links to Gold/Indices/Combo/copier/calculators/performance.
