## 4. Overall

| Metric | Previous design | New design |
|---|---|---|
| Targetable queries covered (exact phrase) | 15 / 52 | 39 / 52 |
| Partial (concept only) | 5 | 8 |
| Not covered | 32 | 5 |
| Non-brand queries sitting at position 11–30 | 12 | each now has its own section or FAQ block |
| Sections with a single keyword focus | 2 (hero, FAQ) | 11 |
| In-body links to Gold / Indices / Combo | 3 | 6 |

**Why the old page plateaued:** it repeated "forex VIP signals" 15 times but had no section for how people actually search — `vip trading signals`, `forex trading vip signals`, `signal vip`, `daily vip signals`, `vip signal group`, `forex vip lounge`, `vip access`. That cluster alone is **~382 impressions/month**, mostly sitting at positions 11–33 where on-page work still moves the needle.

**What still needs work to close the last gaps**

1. Add real Persian/Arabic copy near the support block (rows 20 and 29) instead of only naming the languages.
2. Merge brand + proof into single sentences — e.g. "SureShotFX reviews on Myfxbook and Trustpilot" — to convert the six 🟡 brand rows into ✅.
3. Add "SureShotFX Gold" and "SureShotFX Bot" as explicit pairings, since they are always split today.

Everything else in the targetable set is now covered.
