The basics

What is a prop firm drawdown EA?

A drawdown EA is an Expert Advisor that monitors your account against a loss limit and acts when you reach it. Guardian is built for prop firm challenges: it tracks the daily loss and the overall drawdown, then closes trades or halts trading before the limit is breached.

Most prop firms fail an account when the daily or maximum loss is exceeded. Guardian turns that rule into an automated guard, so a fast losing session does not end the challenge while you are away from the screen.

Features

What does the Guardian drawdown EA do?

Eight features, all pointed at one job: keeping the account inside the prop firm's loss rules.

Daily loss limit

Set the percentage or amount your firm allows for the day. Guardian tracks it from the daily reset.

Overall drawdown limit

A second ceiling for the whole challenge, so a slow bleed cannot breach the maximum loss either.

Real-time monitoring

The EA checks equity and floating loss continuously, not once at the end of the day.

Auto-halt and close all

At the limit, Guardian stops opening new trades and closes positions, so the breach does not happen.

CLOSE command

Send "CLOSE" from Telegram to end all trades immediately, even when you are away from the terminal.

Telegram and MT4/MT5 alerts

Drawdown warnings and account status reach your phone, so you can act before the limit.

Per-day reset

Daily limits reset on the server clock, matching how prop firms calculate the new trading day.

Runs beside other EAs

Guardian manages risk in the background without replacing the strategy or EA on the chart.

Guardian: common questions

How the drawdown EA works, what it needs, and what it cannot do.

What is SSF Guardian?

+

SSF Guardian is a proprietary Expert Advisor for MT4 and MT5 that manages drawdown limits. It tracks daily and overall loss, warns you before the limit and closes or halts trades at the threshold set by your prop firm.

How does it differ from other risk management tools?

+

Guardian is built around prop firm rules: a daily loss limit, an overall drawdown limit and a daily reset. It monitors in real time, stops trading at the limit and sends alerts to Telegram and MT4/MT5.

Do I need technical expertise to use it?

+

No. Installation is an EA attach and a short settings form. If you get stuck, support can connect remotely and finish the first setup for you.

How quickly does it respond to excessive drawdown?

+

It responds in real time. As soon as floating equity reaches the limit you set, Guardian stops opening new trades and closes positions. Execution still depends on your broker and market conditions.

Can I set different limits for different days?

+

You can set a daily limit and an overall limit. The daily limit resets on the server clock, and the overall limit stays for the life of the account or challenge.

Will it interfere with my other EAs or strategy?

+

No. Guardian runs in the background and focuses only on drawdown management. It does not generate entries or replace the EA already on your chart. Check your prop firm's rules on running multiple EAs.

Does it close trades automatically?

+

Yes. When the loss limit is reached, Guardian closes open positions and stops new entries. You can also send the word CLOSE from Telegram to close everything immediately.

Does it work with any prop firm?

+

It works with any prop firm that allows MT4 or MT5 Expert Advisors. Check your firm's rules first, especially the daily loss calculation method, because firms calculate balances and equity differently.

Is Guardian compatible with MT4 and MT5?

+

Yes. The same licence covers MetaTrader 4 and MetaTrader 5 on two accounts, so you can guard a challenge account and a funded account at the same time.

Are updates to Guardian free?

+

Yes. Updates are free for the life of the licence. You will not be asked to repurchase or move to a new tier.

Is Guardian a trade copier?

+

No. Guardian manages drawdown and does not copy signals. If you want signals mirrored to your account, use the SureShotFX Trade Copier. The two products can run together.
